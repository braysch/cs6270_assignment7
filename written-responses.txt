Q1. Why is possible that Update and Delete Widget Requests may fail, even when you were running just one Consumer?

A1: Standard queues use best-effort ordering, which does not guarantee that messages will be delivered in the order they were sent. This means we could possibly attempt to update or delete a widget before it is created.

Q2: How would this possible behavior impact the design of distributed applications that use queues?

A2: Without a strict FIFO queue, it is necessary to handle reordering logic and retry logic so that the system is eventually consistent.
